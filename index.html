Good food. Simpler days.

DailyPrep exists because prepping vegetables is the part of cooking nobody looks forward to. We do that part — properly, hygienically, the same day — so what lands in your kitchen is ready to cook, not ready to chop.

  • No preservatives. Just washed, cut and chilled — nothing added to keep it looking fresh.
  • Keep refrigerated. Every pack travels cold and stays good in your fridge for days, not hours.
  • Washed & ready. Triple-rinsed and drained, so there's nothing left for you to do but cook.
  • Saves real time. Most home cooks spend 20–30 minutes a day on prep alone — we give that back.
